# Cybersecurity Market Gaps For Hackathon Products

## Gap 1: Fragmented Security Visibility For Small Teams

Most SMB teams and student builders use separate tools for:

- Permission/privacy checks
- URL or phishing triage
- Device posture checks
- Incident response guidance

They do not get one prioritized flow from detection to action.

## Gap 2: High Friction Between Detection And Remediation

Many tools produce alerts but not a concrete next-step playbook that non-experts can execute quickly.

## Gap 3: Poor API-First Integration In Early Security Products

Hackathon projects often demo isolated dashboards but fail to expose ingestion/export APIs required by real SOC workflows.

## Chosen Product Direction: NightWatch Sentinel

NightWatch Sentinel addresses all 3 gaps with:

- Unified browser/device/network exposure scan
- Built-in URL threat heuristics
- Gemini-generated actionable remediation plan
- Integration-ready REST event endpoints and JSON export

## Why This Can Scale Beyond A Hackathon

- Modular analyzers can become a marketplace/plugin ecosystem.
- API-first architecture enables SOC/SIEM compatibility.
- AI remediation can evolve into workflow automation and policy enforcement.
